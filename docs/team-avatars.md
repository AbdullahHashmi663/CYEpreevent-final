## Scarf avatar prompt

Create one polished square profile avatar illustration for an orange and royal-blue university event website. Single fictional young adult Pakistani woman, modest opaque royal-blue hijab/scarf fully covering her hair and neck, friendly professional neutral expression, head and shoulders, looking forward, navy and cobalt outfit with a small warm orange accent. Elegant contemporary editorial vector-like raster illustration, soft dimensional shading, tasteful minimal facial details, clean pale blue solid background with subtle orange circle, centered portrait with generous headroom suitable for both square and portrait card crops. Colors #1D4594 #FF4B2B #F0F5FF. No text, no logos, no border, no watermark. Output a single avatar only.
